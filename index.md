# HotLap — Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026 · Developer: dustinsterk

HotLap is a Garmin Connect IQ watch app for lap timing and motorsports
telemetry. This policy explains what data the app accesses, how it is used, and
who receives it. In short: your activity and health data stay on your watch and
in **your own** Garmin Connect account — the developer never receives it.

## Information the app accesses
While you use HotLap, it reads data from your Garmin device to time laps and
show/record telemetry, including:

- GPS location, speed, heading, and elevation
- Heart rate, temperature, pulse oximetry (SpO2), and respiration, where your device provides them
- Motion-derived G-force (computed from GPS), lap and sector times

## How the data is used and stored
- It is used on-device to display your dashboard/timing and to record a standard Garmin activity (FIT file).
- Saved activities are stored on your watch and sync to **your own Garmin Connect account** under your control.
- The developer of HotLap does **not** collect, receive, transmit, or store your location, health, or activity data. Garmin's handling of your Garmin Connect data is governed by [Garmin's Privacy Policy](https://www.garmin.com/privacy/).

## Purchases (KiezelPay)
The optional in-app unlock is handled by **KiezelPay**, a third-party payment
service. To verify a purchase, KiezelPay sends an anonymous device identifier
and the app ID to its servers. HotLap does not collect or process payment card
details, and no location, health, or activity data is sent to KiezelPay or the
developer. See [KiezelPay](https://www.kiezelpay.com/) for details on their
processing.

## What the app does NOT do
- No user accounts or sign-in are created by HotLap.
- No advertising or analytics SDKs.
- Your personal data is never sold or shared for marketing.

## Device permissions and why
- **Positioning (GPS)** — lap timing, speed, track and G-force.
- **Sensor / SensorHistory** — heart rate, temperature, SpO2, respiration.
- **Fit / FitContributor** — record the activity and its custom data fields.
- **Communications** — used only by KiezelPay to verify a purchase.

## Data retention and your choices
Recorded activities live in your Garmin Connect account; you can view, export,
or delete them there at any time. Uninstalling HotLap removes its on-device data.
The app keeps no separate copy of your data off your device.

## Children
HotLap is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect data from
children.
